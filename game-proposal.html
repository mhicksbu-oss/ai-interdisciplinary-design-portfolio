Don't Look Twice

Playable Game / Interactive Narrative

Description

Don't Look Twice is a playable perceptual thriller about Apartment 12—a room missing from every building plan. After receiving a 4:17 A.M. call from their own phone, the player must identify changes in an unstable room and prove which version of them is real.

Timed memory tests lead to consequential story choices, suspense reveals, multiple cliffhanger endings, and a failure path. The game turns attention into survival: miss two changes and the room sends something else home in the player’s place.

Research process

Research The Ratchelor: A Rat Dating Game by Algorat. Find primary sources, identify credited contributors, and summarize what the creators say about the concept, interaction, visuals, and sound. Separate stated intentions from interpretation and identify what cannot be verified.
Case study insight

Compact interactions can build character and emotional stakes through selection, reaction, and consequence.

Playable experience

Enter Apartment 12, survive three timed memory tests, make consequential choices, and uncover one of several cliffhanger endings.

Design decision

Give uncertainty real stakes: identify at least two changes or fail the identity test and try again.
