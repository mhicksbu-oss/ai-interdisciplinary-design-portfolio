Don't Look Twice

Game Proposal / Interaction

Description

Don't Look Twice is a developing interactive narrative game about unreliable perception. The player enters a familiar place that changes whenever they stop looking at it. Each contradiction creates a choice: trust memory, trust the visible evidence, or reinforce a different version of reality.

The intended emotional journey moves from curiosity to doubt and finally to responsibility. The player begins as an observer, but gradually discovers that attention is also a creative act. What they choose to notice—and what they choose to ignore—determines which world becomes permanent.

Research process

Research The Ratchelor: A Rat Dating Game by Algorat. Find primary sources, identify credited contributors, and summarize what the creators say about the concept, interaction, visuals, and sound. Separate stated intentions from interpretation and identify what cannot be verified.
Case study insight

Compact interactions can build character and emotional stakes through selection, reaction, and consequence.

Player journey

Notice a contradiction, compare evidence, test the environment, then commit to one version of reality.

Design decision

Keep research and credits precise while allowing uncertainty to exist inside the fictional experience.
